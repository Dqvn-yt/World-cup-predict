Leakage controls

Every prediction sees only the past

Rolling player and team features are calculated after chronological sorting and shifted before the current match.

Outcome columns, match xG, current events and final tournament aggregates never enter historical model features.

Scalers, imputers and encoders are fitted inside sklearn pipelines on the training period only.
